Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Use the chat assistant in the corner, or contact us directly.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer and cash on delivery for local orders. Card payment can be set up on request — just ask when you request your quote.

How long does it take to build my PC?

Most builds take about 24 hours once we have all the parts in hand. Delivery time on top of that depends on your location.

What does the warranty cover?

Every build comes with a 12-month service warranty covering assembly issues and part failures under normal use. We also offer support after delivery for any questions.

Can I request specific brands or parts?

Absolutely. Tell us the brands or components you prefer and we'll build around them whenever compatible with your budget and goals.

Do you ship outside Romania?

Yes, we can arrange shipping internationally — cost and delivery time depend on your country. Ask us for a shipping quote along with your PC quote.

What if my PC arrives damaged?

Every PC is tested before it leaves us and carefully packaged. If anything is damaged in transit, contact us right away and we'll fix or replace it under warranty at no extra cost.

Do I need to provide my own monitor, keyboard, or mouse?

No — we can include a monitor, keyboard, mouse, or gaming chair in your order. Check our Gear page for our recommended picks by budget.

How do I get a quote?

Fill out the form on our Contact page with your budget and the games you play, and we'll reply with a clear, itemized quote.

Can you just upgrade my current PC instead of building a new one?

Yes — we regularly upgrade existing PCs, including ones bought from a store. We'll tell you exactly what's worth changing for better performance.

What if I don't know my budget yet?

That's fine — tell us what games you want to play and how you plan to use the PC, and we'll suggest a few options at different price points.
